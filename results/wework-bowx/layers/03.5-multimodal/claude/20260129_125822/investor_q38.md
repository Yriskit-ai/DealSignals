## Response

Based on the document, WeWork had already undergone significant business model transformation by the time of this presentation, with several key changes implemented and additional ones planned:

## Changes Already Implemented by 2020-2021:

**Cost Structure Optimization (Page 16):**
- ~$1.1B in functional expense savings (HIGH confidence)
- ~$400M in operational expense savings
- $200M+ savings from 200+ lease exits and amendments
- Exited non-core businesses including conductor, Flatiron School, Meetup, SpaceIQ, Spacious, teem, and Waltz

**Business Model Refocusing (Page 14):**
- Shifted focus to "Core WeWork" - the core leased flexible workspace business
- Moved from mix of small and medium-sized businesses toward primarily Enterprise customers (500+ employees)
- Disciplined focus on cost reduction and path to profitability

## Future Platform Evolution Planned:

**Three-Stage Platform Development (Page 22):**
1. **Space-as-a-Service** - Current core offering providing flexibility of space, time, and cost portability
2. **Digitization of Real Estate** - All Access offering to digitize WeWork spaces and provide ultimate flexibility
3. **Platform** - Third party flexible workspace management services leveraging WeWork's property and technology platform

**Revenue Diversification (Page 25-26):**
- **All Access**: On-demand/subscription access to global network
- **Marketplace**: Tech services, events, HR services, with insurance expected Q1 2021E
- **Platform**: Asset-light management and franchise agreements with landlords
